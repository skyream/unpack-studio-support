Unpack Studio Privacy Policy

On-device files

Compression, extraction and local previews run on your device. Imported copies and results are saved in the app's file directory. You can delete them in the app. Originals and shared copies are managed separately.

Permissions

When you import or capture media, the app receives the items you select or capture through iOS. Manage photo and camera access in Settings.

Wi-Fi transfer and sharing

While Wi-Fi Transfer is open, devices on your local network that can reach the displayed address can access the shared directory. Use a trusted network and close the transfer when finished. Files sent through the share sheet are handled by the recipient or service you choose.

Subscriptions and support

Apple processes purchases. Unpack Studio uses StoreKit to verify your Pro entitlement on the device. When you contact us by email, we use your email address and message to respond.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email gameskydream@gmail.com.
